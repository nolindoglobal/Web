From Local Markets to the World: The Nolindo Global Story

A family business rooted in Jonggol, built with local farmers, and guided by a commitment to dependable quality.

By NOLINDO GLOBALPT. Nolindo Global Internasional

Indonesian banana leaves, grown and prepared with care in West Java.

Banana leaves rarely get the spotlight. Yet from steamed dishes in South and Southeast Asia to tamales in Latin America, they are used to wrap, steam, grill and serve food in kitchens around the world. For my family, they have been something more: a livelihood, a craft, and the foundation of PT Nolindo Global Internasional.

Where it began

Our story starts with a family business. Since 2011, my family has been in the banana leaf business, selling in local markets to meet the daily demand of Jabodetabek, the Greater Jakarta area. It was honest, hard, everyday work, and it shaped how we think about the trade: freshness and reliability come first.

Rooted in Jonggol

Today, our family manages almost 130,500 square meters of land (about 13 hectares) in the mountainous area of Jonggol, West Java. We do not do this alone. We work hand in hand with local farmers, and together we harvest an average of 5 to 8 tons of banana leaves per day. Behind every ton are people: the farmers who tend the plants and harvest the leaves.

Raising the standard

Supplying local markets and supplying the world are different challenges. International importers, distributors and food manufacturers expect leaves that look, feel and perform the same in every shipment.

For a buyer, banana leaves are not a commodity to be chosen on price alone. Size, color, condition and cleanliness all matter, and so does the confidence that next month’s shipment will match last month’s.

That is why I began raising our quality to meet global market standards. The work starts in the field. I trained our farmers in plant care and in how the leaves are harvested, because consistent quality cannot be added at the end; it has to be built in from the very beginning. Training and close cooperation with the people who grow the leaves are now at the heart of how PT Nolindo works.

What this means for our partners

The result is a supply built on three things:

  • Consistency: leaves of dependable quality, shipment after shipment.
  • Flexibility: fresh and frozen banana leaves, custom sizes and packaging, and low minimum order quantities, so buyers can start at a scale that suits them.
  • A known source: leaves from land our family manages and farmers we work with directly.
Fresh leaves suit buyers who need product quickly; frozen leaves give importers and distributors more room to plan storage and shipping.

Looking ahead

From a family market business to an exporter serving international buyers, our journey has been guided by the same values from the start: care for the land, respect for the farmers, and a commitment to quality that buyers can rely on. If you are an importer, distributor, wholesaler or food producer looking for a dependable banana leaf supplier from Indonesia, I would be glad to connect.
